Hi, I’m Chen Tian. 👋

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Information Systems at the Goizueta Business School, Emory University, expecting to graduate in May 2026. I am fortunate to be advised by [Prof. Ramnath Chellappa](https://nimbusprof.com/).

My research lies at the intersection of digital technology and social welfare, with a particular focus on analyzing and evaluating AI-related infrastructure and systems, as well as their environmental and economic implications.

Currently, my work focuses on two main areas:

- Digital Infrastructure, Technology Policy, and Green IT

- Digital Sovereignty, Algorithm Transparency, and AI Incidents

I hold a Master’s degree in Management Science and Engineering from Zhejiang University and a Bachelor’s degree in Information Management and Information Systems from Nanjing University.
